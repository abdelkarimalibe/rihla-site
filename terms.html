The service

Rihla connects Muslim diners with halal restaurants in New York. Restaurants get a QR counter card and a page on this site. Diners scan the card to see the restaurant's verified halal status.

The free pilot

The pilot is free for 30 days, with no contract and no card required. At the end of the 30 days, Rihla sends the restaurant a report showing scans, page views, and engagement through its QR card. The paid tier is $149 per month for Basic or $250 per month for Full, or $2,700 per year paid upfront for Full, cancel anytime on a monthly plan, and only starts if the restaurant agrees after seeing the report.

First-visit offers

Any first-visit offer shown through a Rihla card is agreed with that specific restaurant. Rihla does not set a discount on a restaurant's behalf.

Verification

A Rihla listing reflects the check made on the date shown for that restaurant. Restaurants must keep their halal information accurate and tell Rihla if anything changes, such as a new meat supplier or a lapsed certificate. If information becomes out of date, Rihla may update or remove the listing.

Information as-is

Restaurant information on this site is provided to help diners decide, but menus, hours, and halal status can change. If you are unsure about anything, confirm with the restaurant directly before ordering.
