The service

Rihla connects Muslim diners with halal restaurants in New York. Restaurants get a QR counter card and a page on this site. Diners scan the card and get 15% off their first visit.

The free pilot

The pilot is free for 30 days, with no contract and no card required. At the end of the 30 days, Rihla brings the restaurant a report showing scans, page views, and engagement through its QR card. The paid tier is $79 per month, cancel anytime, and only starts if the restaurant agrees after seeing the report.

The discount

Diners show the Rihla screen to their server to claim 15% off their first visit. Restaurants in the pilot agree to honour that discount for first visits through the card.

Verification

A Rihla listing reflects the check made on the date shown for that restaurant. Restaurants must keep their halal information accurate and tell Rihla if anything changes, such as a new meat supplier or a lapsed certificate. If information becomes out of date, Rihla may update or remove the listing.

Information as-is

Restaurant information on this site is provided to help diners decide, but menus, hours, and halal status can change. If you are unsure about anything, confirm with the restaurant directly before ordering.
