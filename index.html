Questions

How Rihla works.

For diners

How does Rihla check that a restaurant is halal?

Every Rihla listing shows how it was checked and when. Hand-Checked means we visited in person. Document-Verified means we reviewed the halal certificate and a recent supplier invoice. Community-Reported means a diner told us, and it is marked unconfirmed. Every check shows its date and expires after 12 months. Owners start by claiming their spot on this page and sending their halal certificate and a recent supplier invoice; we check them, then the mark goes live.

Is the NYC halal list free?

Yes. The list is free, and we never send spam. Leave your email at the top of this page to get it.

Which parts of New York does Rihla cover?

Rihla is verifying the 50 best halal spots in New York. The pilot is underway in Queens, in Astoria.

Do I need an app?

No. At a Rihla-verified spot, scan the QR card on the counter. You see how the halal claim was checked, and any first-visit offer that restaurant has agreed. Then show the screen to your server.

For restaurant owners

What does Rihla cost a restaurant?

The first 30 days are free. No card required, no contract. After that it is $149 a month for Basic, or $250 a month for Full, or $2,700 a year for Full paid yearly, and only if it's working for you. Flat prices: no per-cover fees and no paying per click.

What does a restaurant get?

A QR card for your counter, a verified page that Google and AI can read, and a monthly report that shows your scans. See a demo of the monthly report →

What is the Halal Visibility Check?

A free check of whether diners and AI can tell your restaurant is halal. We check it by hand and call you with your grade and three fixes. See a sample grade →

Do you have to visit my restaurant?

Not necessarily. Most restaurants are Document-Verified: you send your halal certificate and a recent supplier invoice, we review them, and the mark goes live. In-person Hand-Checked visits are for the corridor proof, not the default.

Why does the mark get more valuable over time?

Every verified restaurant makes the mark more trusted, for diners and for AI. The value sits in the accumulated, re-checked history, not in any single listing, so it compounds as more restaurants join.

What makes Rihla different from a free halal directory?

Directories list restaurants. Rihla checks them, shows how and when each was checked, and re-checks every 12 months. A listing is a claim; a Rihla mark is a check.
