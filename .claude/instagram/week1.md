**Length:** 28.0s across 10 beats at 165 wpm, target 30s. No flags.

### Spoken script

```
10 Astoria restaurants. 30 days. Prove me wrong.
My bet? This card brings Moka and Co new diners.
It sits on the counter, you scan it,
```
