## Style notes

- Use contractions in spoken scripts ("you've", "isn't", "I'm"). A second
  draft round dropped them all and read stiff out loud.

- Opens on the place or the shared moment, not on the brand. Rihla comes in
  mid-script, never in the first line.
- Uses real street and station names. Keep them, they are the specificity.
- Speaks for the audience ("you know this moment") and then answers it.
- Captions end on a question to the audience ("Who's missing?", "Where do you
  want us to check next?").
- 4 hashtags, always including #HalalNYC and #Astoria. Under the 5 cap.
- Writes em dashes in drafts. Swap them for commas or full stops before
  posting (`/ig-human` does this automatically).
