Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

What we collect

When you visit Rihla, Google Analytics counts page views and QR scans, including which restaurant's QR code was scanned. If you fill in a form on the site, we receive the details you type, such as your name, restaurant name, email address, and phone number. That is everything.

What we use it for

Analytics lets us show restaurants how many customers came through their QR card, in the monthly proof report. Form details are used to contact restaurant owners about their free pilot, and to contact diners who asked for the NYC list. Nothing else.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits, but only if you accept them in the cookie banner on the site. If you decline, we do not store analytics cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser data for this site and choosing again when the banner reappears.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to show you ads.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your form details at any time by emailing rihla.enquiries@gmail.com. We will confirm once it is done.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to rihla.enquiries@gmail.com.
