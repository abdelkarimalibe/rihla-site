Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026.

What we collect

When you visit Rihla, Google Analytics counts page views, QR scans and clicks on links in our emails, including which restaurant's QR code or email link it was. It also counts which buttons and links are clicked, when a form is sent, and which campaign link first brought you to the site. Google Analytics is never sent your name, email address or phone number. It does receive the restaurant name typed into the Halal Visibility Check box, and the neighbourhood chosen on the claim form.

If you fill in a form on the site, we receive the details you type, such as your name, restaurant name, email address, and phone number. That is everything.

What we use it for

Analytics lets us show restaurants how many customers came through their QR card, in the monthly proof report. It also shows us which pages, buttons and campaigns bring people in, so we can improve the site. Form details are used to contact restaurant owners about their free pilot, and to contact diners who asked for the NYC list. Nothing else.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to count visits, but only if you accept them in the cookie banner on the site. If you decline, we do not store analytics cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser data for this site and choosing again when the banner reappears.

Your browser also keeps a few small notes for this site that are not cookies, such as your answer to the banner and the campaign link that first brought you here.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to show you ads.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your form details at any time by emailing rihla.enquiries@gmail.com. We will confirm once it is done.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to rihla.enquiries@gmail.com.
